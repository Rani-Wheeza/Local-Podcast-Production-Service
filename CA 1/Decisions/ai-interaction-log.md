# AI Interaction Log

## Purpose

This log records representative examples of how I used ChatGPT while preparing the CA1 requirements and domain analysis dossier for Scenario 29: Local Podcast Production Service. It describes the assistance requested, how I considered the responses and how the suggestions influenced my work.

| Activity | Assistance requested | How I used or evaluated the output |
|---|---|---|
| Scenario interpretation | Help interpret Scenario 29 and organise the main problems, stakeholders, objectives and system boundaries. | Used the discussion to structure my analysis. I checked the resulting points against the scenario to distinguish stated information from my own interpretations. |
| Requirements analysis | Review the requirements for clarity, consistency and possible gaps. | Considered suggestions for improving the wording and identifying unresolved questions. I retained the distinction between confirmed requirements and matters requiring stakeholder clarification. |
| Business rules and uncertainty | Help distinguish confirmed business rules from assumptions and inferred risks. | Reviewed statements that were too definite and revised them where the scenario did not provide sufficient evidence. For example, possible consequences of missing production information were treated as inferred risks rather than confirmed outcomes. |
| Use cases and traceability | Review the relationship between requirements, actors, use cases, business rules and other analysis artefacts. | Used the feedback to check consistency across sections and identify links that needed to be verified against my requirements and decision records. |
| Gherkin scenarios | Help structure Gherkin feature files and express successful, alternative and failure scenarios. | Used the suggestions to refine the scenarios and their traceability tags. I also checked the Cucumber.js output and recognised that the feature file was parsed, but its steps remained undefined because step definitions had not been implemented. |
| Domain modelling and diagrams | Discuss candidate domain concepts and get guidance on preparing and saving PlantUML diagrams. | Used the discussion to review the concepts and relationships in my model and to organise the diagram source files and rendered images. The model remained subject to review against the scenario. |
| Repository organisation | Get guidance on organising Markdown documents, requirements, feature files, diagrams and decision records in the project repository. | Applied the relevant guidance when preparing the project files. I checked file names, folder locations and references so that the report and repository artefacts could be located consistently. |
| Reflection and AI declaration | Help structure the reflection and describe the use of AI in the assignment. | Reviewed the suggested wording and adapted it to reflect the decisions made during the analysis, the limitations of the work and my actual use of AI. |

## Evaluation and responsibility

I treated AI responses as suggestions rather than as authoritative sources about the scenario. Where a suggestion was useful, I considered how it could improve my analysis; where a statement was unsupported or too definite, it needed to be checked or revised. The scenario and assignment brief remained the basis for the work.

I am responsible for reviewing the submitted requirements, diagrams, Gherkin scenarios, references and other artefacts to ensure that they accurately represent my analysis. This log records representative examples of AI assistance rather than a complete transcript of every interaction.
