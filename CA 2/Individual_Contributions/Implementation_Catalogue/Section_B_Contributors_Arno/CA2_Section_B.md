## Problem statement

Rani’s problem statement describes scattered records and the need to restrict access to sensitive information (E1). This section adds details about contributors and consent from the Evidence Document Source Material for Section B (E2 to E7 and E18 to E21).

Consent can be given in three forms (E19 and E2):

1.  On paper.

2.  By email.

3.  Verbally at the start of a recording.

Before publication, staff must check paper forms, emails and recordings for consent. These separate checks can make it difficult to confirm whether all necessary consent is recorded (E3).
A completed draft cannot be published if contributor confirmation, a permission or an editorial decision is still outstanding (E4 and E18). Contributors also need to know how their recordings will be used and how they will be credited (E7 and E13).
Access to sensitive contributor information must reflect production roles (E18). Incorrect access could cause privacy harm and loss of contributor trust. This is an identified risk, not a confirmed event (E21 and E5).
The analysis identifies these information and access needs. It does not confirm that a privacy breach has occurred or state how often the problems occur (E1).
